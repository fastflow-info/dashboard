FASTSIGNS · HELM

A production board for five centres across two districts, built on live CoreBridge data. Every figure on this page is work that is open right now, not a forecast and not a record of what has already shipped.

What the money figures are

Every price here is the product price before tax, so a figure matches the subtotal on the order in CoreBridge rather than the invoice total, which carries tax on top of it.

An order that is partly built shows only the products still open. It reads lower than its invoice by the value of whatever has already been finished, which is the point: this board counts work outstanding, not work sold.

What you are reading

Open value is the money standing in the shop today. Overdue is the part of it that has passed its promised date. Due today is the part that is promised for this date. The three overlap by design, so they will not add up to the total.

Where it comes from

Two CoreBridge tenants, read live and merged into one book. A line item counts once, under the centre that owns it. Anything already built drops out at the source, which is why a finished product still shows in the progress ring but never in a total.

How often it moves

The page refreshes every ten minutes and on every scope change. The two views that carry history, the trend line and the stalled board, are recorded once a visit and kept in this browser, so they build up on the machine you read them on.

How to read the colour

Claret is time that has run out. Navy is time still in hand. Each centre keeps its own identifier colour wherever centres are being compared, and the ink marks whatever is due on the day you are looking at.

Opening Helm to a specific view

Helm normally opens at the top showing every centre. You can make it open already filtered to a slice of the board by adding an instruction to the end of the web address — useful for wall screens and standups. Add ? and an instruction; stack more than one with &.

?center=Torrance
Opens showing only that centre. Any centre name works — Long Beach, Santa Ana, Torrance, Tarzana, Thousand Oaks — or valley / southbay for a whole district.
?due=late
Opens already filtered by urgency. Choices: late (overdue only), today (due today), act (needs action), all (everything).
?kiosk=1
Wall-screen mode. Holds the board fully open with no scrolling needed, and locks the schedule so a stray touch can't shift the dates for everyone reading it.
#repSec
Jumps straight down to a scorecard on open. #repSec for By Salesperson, #ctrSec for By Centre, #stallSec for Hasn't Moved.
Examples
helm.html?kiosk=1 A shared wall screen showing all centres, no scrolling. helm.html?kiosk=1&center=Torrance&due=act A screen on the Torrance floor showing only their work that needs action. helm.html?due=late#repSec A standup link: overdue work only, opened straight to the salesperson scorecard.
